# First operations hire

<!-- SAMPLE CONTENT: replace with the licensed coaching corpus. Written for this demo only. -->

Hire your first operations person when you spend more than ten hours a week on scheduling, invoicing and follow-ups. Hire for reliability and process over experience in your industry.

Run a paid trial project of one to two weeks before committing. Give them a written scorecard with three outcomes and a review date so expectations are clear from day one.

Keep a shared document of recurring tasks and update it every time something is handed over.
