# Time audit

<!-- SAMPLE CONTENT: replace with the licensed coaching corpus. Written for this demo only. -->

A time audit tracks every work activity for two weeks and scores each one by energy (drains or energises) and by value (low or high dollars per hour). The goal is to see where hours actually go instead of where you think they go.

Sort the audited tasks into four groups: high value and energising, high value and draining, low value and energising, low value and draining. The draining, low value group is the first to hand off.

Repeat the audit every quarter. Tasks that once belonged to you often become delegable as the company grows.
