# Delegation levels

<!-- SAMPLE CONTENT: replace with the licensed coaching corpus. Written for this demo only. -->

Delegate in stages rather than all at once. At level one the other person researches and reports back while you decide. At level two they recommend an action and you approve it. At level three they act and tell you afterwards. At level four they own the outcome completely.

Move a task up one level only after the person has handled it correctly three times in a row. Document the task as a short checklist before handing it over, so the process lives outside your head.

Expect a quality dip in the first weeks. Judge the result against 80 percent of your own standard, not 100 percent.
