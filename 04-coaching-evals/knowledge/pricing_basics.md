# Pricing basics

<!-- SAMPLE CONTENT: replace with the licensed coaching corpus. Written for this demo only. -->

Price against the value delivered to the customer, not against your costs. Ask what the problem costs the customer each month and anchor on a fraction of that number.

Offer three tiers. The middle tier should be the one you want most people to choose. Raise prices when more than about half of prospects accept without negotiating, because that signals the price is too low.

Announce increases to existing customers early and give them a short window to lock in the old rate.
